# Contributing

This repository is maintained by a single author for their own use, and is not open to contributions.

## Pull requests

Pull requests are not accepted and will be closed without review, whatever their content.

## Forks

The code is released under the [MIT No Attribution License (MIT-0)](LICENSE). You are free to fork it, modify it, and publish
and maintain your own version under the terms of that license. Forks are independent: changes made in
a fork will not be merged back into this repository.

## Issues

Bug reports may be opened as issues. They are read, but no response, fix or timeline is guaranteed.
